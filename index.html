About Me

Engineer • Researcher • Builder

Machine Learning Engineer at mVizn Pte. Ltd., engineering scalable GenAI multi-agent platforms for construction project management and procurement, with CrewAI, MCP tool-calling, RAG pipelines, LoRA fine-tuning, and automated reporting. Also leads 3D Scan-to-BIM work, building point-cloud semantic segmentation, MLflow/Airflow MLOps workflows, FastAPI backends, and Docker/GCP deployments.

Awarded a PhD in Computer Vision and AI from the Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering Department of Jadavpur University (JU), Kolkata. Researched AI/ML-based detection, classification, segmentation and prediction algorithms using Deep Learning and Computer Vision for neurological disorders via radiological and histopathological imaging.

Before research, worked at Mercedes-Benz R&D India (MBRDI) developing standalone tools with Python, PyQT and MySQL, and automating data quality checks. Also has freelancing experience building and hosting a web platform for a Canadian client.

Deeply interested in Physical AI, Generative AI, and Robotics. Currently exploring LeRobot, ROS2 and CrewAI. Avid photographer, mythology enthusiast, casual gamer, and hardcore foodie.
